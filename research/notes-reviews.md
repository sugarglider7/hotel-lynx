## What guests criticise (→ things the site must NOT promise)
- **Noise**: call to prayer from Mosquée Mohamed V (early morning; Ramadan nights), street noise till ~23:00, motorbikes, rooms on the parking side; thin windows/doors rattling in wind. → never "quiet", "peaceful", "soundproof". OK to say earplugs/ask for a room away from the street? Only if owner confirms such rooms exist.
- **No lift** (Booking: "Upper floors accessible by stairs only"; Boris CZ, Molina ES, Maykin BE; Colin UK swapped to a ground-floor room). → state it plainly; useful honesty for guests with heavy bags/mobility needs.
- **Dated furniture / decor** ("Dated hotel… old furniture", "1970s retro interior", "rooms are becoming a little shabby"). → never "modern", "renovated", "boutique".
- **Bathrooms**: open walk-in shower without curtain/tray → water on the floor; occasional weak/cold hot water (mornings, winter); one small soap, no shampoo; occasional blocked drain. → never "rain shower", "toiletries provided".
- **No breakfast** (see verdict in SOURCE_OF_TRUTH). **No pool**, **no fridge**, **no kettle**, few sockets.
- Isolated: night arrival refused/door closed (Hamza, Morocco, Aug 2026, score 1) → advise late arrivals to message ahead even though desk is "24-hour" on Booking.
