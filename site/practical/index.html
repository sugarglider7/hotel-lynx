Getting here

Finding us.

  1. 01By car

    Park for free in the public spaces right by the hotel. They’re public, so we can’t reserve one for you.

  2. 02From the airport

    Al Massira airport is about 24 km away: about 30 minutes by taxi, traffic permitting. Petits taxis don’t serve the airport — ask us about booking one when you write.

  3. 03By bus

    The CTM bus station and the Supratours buses are each about 5 minutes away by petit taxi.

  4. 04From the beach

    23 minutes on foot from the beach promenade, or about 4 minutes by petit taxi.

House rules

Short and simple.

  • Check-in 12:00–22:00

    Arriving after 22:00? Call reception before you set off.

  • Check-out by 12:00

    Ask at the desk if you need a little longer.

    Every room. There’s no smoking room.

    Pets are not allowed.

Questions

Before you book.

Do you serve breakfast?

No. The first cafés are 3–4 minutes from the door and Talborjt is full of them — see where.

Is the parking really free?

Yes. Public spaces right by the hotel, free of charge. They’re public, so we can’t reserve one for you.

Is there a lift?

No — the upper floors are by the stairs only. Heavy bags? Ask at the desk for a hand. If stairs are hard for you, ask for a lower floor when you write.

Can I arrive after 22:00?

Check-in is from 12:00 to 22:00. If you’ll be later, call reception before you travel and check with us first — the desk is staffed 24 hours.

Can you book an airport taxi?

Ask us when you write and reception will try to arrange one. The ride is about 30 minutes; petits taxis don’t go to the airport.

Is the Wi-Fi free?

Yes, free. Guests rate it 9.5 on Booking.com.

Is it quiet?

We’re in the middle of Talborjt: the mosque is across the square and the streets are lively in the evening. Light sleeper? Mention it when you write.

Is there a pool?

No pool and no gym. The beach promenade is 23 minutes on foot.

Can I get laundry done?

Ask at reception.

How much is a room?

It depends on the dates. Ask for a room or call reception for today’s price, or see our rates on Booking.com.

Anything else?

Reception is staffed 24 hours. Call, or ask for a room and add your question.
