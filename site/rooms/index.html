In every room

What every room has.

  • Air-conditioning

    A wall unit in every room.

  • Your own shower room

    Walk-in shower, basin, toilet. No bathtubs.

  • Towels and fresh linen

    Ready when you arrive.

  • Flat-screen TV

    Satellite and cable channels.

  • Free Wi-Fi

    Guests rate it 9.5 on Booking.com.

  • Wake-up call

    Ask at reception the night before.

  • Non-smoking

    Every room, no exceptions.

  • Up the stairs

    There’s no lift — see below.

What we don’t do.

  • Breakfast

    We don’t serve it. The first cafés are 3–4 minutes from the door — see where.

  • A lift

    Upper floors by the stairs only. Heavy bags? Ask at the desk for a hand.

  • Pool or gym

    The beach promenade is 23 minutes on foot, about 4 by taxi.

  • Extra beds

    None. Three of you? Book the Triple.

  • Smoking rooms

    Every room is non-smoking.
